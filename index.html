Abstract: M. Imansyah Basudewa, S.T., M.T. is a Computer Engineering Graduate Researcher at Memorial University of Newfoundland. Combining rigorous mathematical theory with over four years of telecommunications R&D and engineering project management experience, his research investigates the algebraic and topological structures linking classical channel coding (Hamming Distance Cubes, Triangle Hamming Codes, Massive MIMO, and Coherent Optical Fiber) with fault tolerant quantum computing (2D Surface Codes, 7 Qubit Steane Codes, Unitary Group Topologies, and Quantum Cryptography).
